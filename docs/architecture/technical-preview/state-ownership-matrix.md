read-only derivative that can be rebuilt from its owner.

## Writer rules

- A service may call another owner but may not write that owner’s tables or
  artifact namespace directly.
- Database triggers are not a second domain writer; domain invariants remain in
  the owning service and transaction.
- Projections and caches are disposable. They never grant approval or
  eligibility.
- Audit events are emitted by the same transaction that owns the domain change;
  a separate asynchronous audit writer cannot create a success record for a
  failed transaction.
- Compatibility adapters delegate to exactly one owner. They never dual-write
  legacy memory and persistent state.
- Existing `ir`, `ground`, `geom`, `app/orchestration.py`, and `export` modules
  are extended or wrapped. A new frontend or service cannot reproduce their
  engineering rules.
